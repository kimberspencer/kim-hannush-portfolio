About

The work is search strategy and content governance inside large institutions — understanding how information is organized, where it breaks down and how to fix it at scale. The job is reading those systems clearly and building the frameworks that make them hold.

My first SEO work was for a municipal skate park in Oviedo — two miles from UCF, invisible to the students who would have loved it like I did. I found organic ways to get the right people to it. Two decades later, the puzzle scales but the shape doesn't change.

That through-line — systems, people, information and the gaps between them — has run through every role since. It's why I now govern search performance and information architecture across a large-scale university web ecosystem, shaping how hundreds of sites are organized, found and understood — and why I'm watching closely as AI-driven discovery and zero-click behavior reshape how institutions earn visibility, hold authority and sustain impact.
